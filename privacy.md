# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** July 2, 2026

## Introduction

San Jose Puncture Society is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

## Information We Collect

The app collects only the geographic location of bicycle flat tire reports that users choose to submit.

The submitted location is used solely to identify areas where cyclists frequently experience flat tires. This information helps build a community dataset that may be used to identify debris hotspots and areas where bike infrastructure or street maintenance could be improved.

The app does **not** collect:

- Your name
- Your email address
- Your phone number
- User accounts
- Payment information
- Contacts
- Photos
- Any personally identifiable information

## How Information Is Used

Anonymous flat tire location data is used to:

- Display reported flat tire locations on the map.
- Identify recurring puncture hotspots.
- Support analysis of cycling infrastructure and street conditions.
- Improve the usefulness of the application.

## Data Sharing

Anonymous location reports may be displayed publicly within the app and may be shared in aggregate to support research, advocacy, or discussions related to cycling safety and infrastructure.

No personally identifiable information is collected or shared.

## Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect stored data. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

## Children's Privacy

The app is not intended for children under the age of 13.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
